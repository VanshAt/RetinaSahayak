## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 3 implements an explainable AI pipeline and automated medical reporting engine. The explainability layer produces Grad-CAM++ visual decision-support heatmaps that strictly reflect model activation regions without claiming lesion detection. A ReportLab-based 2-page A4 medical report compiles patient context, dual fundus views, image quality assessment (IQA), diagnostic probabilities, Grad-CAM++ visualizations, clinical rationales, ophthalmologist verification QR codes, and regulatory disclaimers.

All operations enforce health-center level multi-tenant access control and write tamper-evident records to the audit log.

### Key Structural Verifications:
1. **Rejected Image Handling:** Case 7 clearly displays the warning banner: *"AI grading was not performed because the fundus image did not meet clinical quality standards."* Recapture guidance is provided.
2. **Uncertainty Routing:** Case 6 clearly displays *"The model confidence is below the safe screening threshold. This case has been routed for ophthalmologist review."*
3. **Medical Disclaimers:** All reports include the CDSS disclaimer: *"Presumptive AI screening observation. Not an autonomous medical diagnosis. Must be reviewed and verified by a licensed ophthalmologist before any clinical treatment."*
4. **Audit Trail:** Every call to `GET /report` or `GET /explainability` automatically writes a structured entry into `audit_logs` containing `user_id`, `user_role`, `action`, `resource_id`, and `status`.
5. **Multi-Tenant Isolation:** Workers from another facility attempting to access reports or explainability receive HTTP `403 Forbidden`.
